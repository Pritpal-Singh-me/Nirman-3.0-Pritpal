About The Movement

DRIPHAUS™ started in a college dorm room with one idea — streetwear shouldn't cost a fortune but should still hit different. We design for the culture, by the culture. No investors. No sellouts. Just raw creativity.

Why DRIPHAUS hits different:

  • Every piece is limited to 100 units — true exclusivity.
  • Ethically sourced fabrics. Heavyweight quality only.
  • Designed in India, inspired by global street culture.
